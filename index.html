Privacy Policy — Marcus Personal AI (WHOOP Integration)

Last updated: 2026-09-12

This policy covers how Marcus Personal AI ("this application") uses data obtained through its integration with the WHOOP API. Marcus Personal AI is a personal, single-user software project; it is not a commercial product and does not offer accounts to other users.

What WHOOP data we access

With your explicit authorization through WHOOP's own OAuth consent screen, this application reads:

  • Sleep data
  • Recovery data
  • Cycle / Strain data
  • Workout data
  • Basic profile data (name/user ID only), used solely to verify that API responses belong to the authorized account — never displayed, stored long-term, or used for any other purpose

What we do with it

Data is used only to generate personal health and wellness summaries and review-only observations for the account owner, through a "Personal Operations Agent" feature (for example, a daily summary of recent sleep, recovery, strain, and workout trends).

All output is review-only: the application never takes an automatic action, makes a medical diagnosis, or asserts authority on the basis of this data. A human reviews everything before it is acted on.

What we do not do

  • We do not write to, modify, or delete any data in your WHOOP account.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, in any form.
  • We do not send your real WHOOP health data to any third-party or external AI model. Only a fixed, local, deterministic component processes it.
  • We do not automatically promote or persist this data into any long-term memory or authority store without a separate, explicit acceptance step.
  • We do not use this data for any financial or trading purpose.
  • We do not import your full historical WHOOP data. Access is limited to small, recent, bounded reads.

Retention and storage

WHOOP data is retained only as long as needed to produce the summaries described above, in a local, non-public data store.

OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored separately from any health data, encrypted at rest, on the account owner's own device, and are never committed to any public code repository.

Revoking access

You may revoke this application's access to your WHOOP account at any time from your WHOOP account settings, or by using WHOOP's own OAuth access-revocation mechanism. Once revoked, this application can no longer read your WHOOP data.
